## 2. Clinical Role Certification Pathways

### 2.1 Critical Care Registered Nurse (RN_CCU)

**Primary Pathway:**
- Required base credential: `RN-BASE-2023`
- Required emergency response: `BLS-2024` (renewed every 2 years), `ACLS-2024` (renewed every 2 years)
- Specialty certification: `CCRN-2024` (renewed every 3 years)

**Recommended Study Pattern for CCRN:**
- 1.5 to 2 hours daily focused study
- Total study target: 30 hours minimum
- Weekly assessment checkpoints
- Target 75 percent practice score before scheduling the exam
- Recommended preparation timeline: 6 to 8 weeks for nurses with 3 or more years critical care experience

### 2.2 Hospital Pharmacist (PHARM_HOSP)

**Primary Pathway:**
- Required base credential: `PHARM-D-2024`
- Specialty certification: `BCPS-2024` (Board Certified Pharmacotherapy Specialist) — renewed every 7 years

**Recommended Study Pattern for BCPS:**
- 2 to 3 hours daily focused study during preparation period
- Total study target: 40 hours minimum
- Structured study groups recommended for higher pass rates
- Recommended preparation timeline: 10 to 12 weeks

---

### 2.3 Medical Laboratory Technician (MLT_LAB)

**Primary Pathway:**
- Required base credential: `MLT-ASCP-2023`
- Recertification: `MLT-ASCP-2026` (renewed every 3 years)

**Recommended Study Pattern for MLT-ASCP Recertification:**
- 1 hour daily focused study
- Total study target: 25 hours minimum
- Emphasis on quality control standards and updated procedures
- Recommended preparation timeline: 4 to 6 weeks

## 5. Assessment Readiness Criteria

Synthetic recommendation: A learner should consider scheduling an exam attempt when all of the following are true:

1. Practice score average is at or above the certification's minimum passing score
2. Study hours completed are at or above the certification's recommended hours
3. All prerequisites are current and not expired
4. At least one full mock assessment has been completed within the last 14 days
5. The learner reports a self-confidence rating of 7 or higher on a 10-point scale
